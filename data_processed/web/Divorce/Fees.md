# Fees

### Fees

  

##### A. List of Court Fees

Note: Payment of all fees must be made through the SYC Portal. The modes of payment are PayNow and eNETS/Credit Card. The filing fee of any application or document which is returned or rejected by the Court Registry is non-refundable. A returned application or document may be resubmitted as part of the same filing transaction without further payment of fees.
