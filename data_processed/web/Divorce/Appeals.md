## Appeals

### Order made by Registrar

An appeal against a direction or order of the registrar may be commenced by filing in the SYC Portal a notice of appeal not later than 14 days from the date of the direction or order. You (known as the appellant) must serve a copy of the notice of appeal on every other party to the appeal within 3 days of filing the notice of appeal. The appeal will be heard by a president of the Syariah Court.  
  
Before you decide to appeal, please seek legal advice on the merits of your proposed course of action.  
  
The form for notice of appeal is found [here](https://syariahcourt.gov.sg/-/media/Project/Syariah-Court/Public-Website/Resources/Forms/Named-Forms/3_4/3_4_19/Form-52-Notice-of-Appeal-against-Registrar-decision-or-order.docx).

### Order made by President

An appeal against a decision of the Syariah Court may be commenced by filing with the Appeal Board a notice of appeal within 30 days from the date of the decision of the Court. You (known as the appellant) must serve a copy of the notice of appeal on every other party to the appeal within 3 days of filing the notice of appeal. The appeal will be heard by an Appeal Board.   
  
Before you decide to appeal, please seek legal advice on the merits of your proposed course of action.   
  
The Appeal Board is located at:
